*Last updated: 16 November 2026*

This blog is written and run by me, Mawumefa Kendeh. I want to know whether people are reading it and which posts are useful, but I want to collect as little as possible to do that. This page explains exactly what is collected, by whom, and how you can say no.

## Visitor counting with GoatCounter (always on)

**GoatCounter** counts page views on every visit. It does not set cookies and does not store anything on your device.

For each page view it records the page you opened, the site that sent you here (for example LinkedIn or a search engine), your browser and operating system, your screen size, and your country. Your country is worked out from your internet (IP) address, and the IP address itself is not stored. None of this is used to identify you or follow you to other sites.

You can read more in [GoatCounter's privacy policy](https://www.goatcounter.com/help/privacy).

## Google Analytics (only if you click Yes)

**Google Analytics** gives me more detail, such as how long people stay and which posts they read in a row. It only loads if you click **Yes** on the cookie banner. If you click **No**, or ignore the banner, it never loads and nothing is sent to Google.

If you click Yes, Google Analytics:

- sets two cookies in your browser, `_ga` and `_ga_PVKTQZCRJG`, which last up to two years and let it recognise the same browser from page to page and visit to visit
- sends Google the pages you view, how you arrived, your device and browser, and your approximate location

I have turned off Google's optional data sharing settings, and I have accepted Google's Data Processing Terms for this account. You can read [how Google uses this information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Changing your choice

Your choice is saved in your browser so the banner does not keep coming back. You can change it at any time with the **Cookie settings** link at the bottom of every page, or here:

<a href="#" data-cookie-settings>Change my cookie settings</a>

If you switch from Yes to No, the Google Analytics cookies are removed from your browser. Clearing your browser's site data also resets your choice, and the banner will ask again.

## Comments

Comments on posts are provided by **Disqus**. When the comment section loads at the bottom of a post, Disqus sets its own cookies and handles any comment you post under [its own privacy policy](https://help.disqus.com/en/articles/1717103-disqus-privacy-policy).

## Fonts and site files

Like most websites, this blog loads fonts and some site files from other services (Google Fonts and the jsDelivr content network). They receive your IP address as part of delivering those files to your browser.
