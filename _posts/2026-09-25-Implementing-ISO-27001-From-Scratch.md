## Let's Implement ISO 27001 from Scratch!

*An introduction to the series, and the map I wish someone had handed me.*

I've spent the better part of eight years in Governance, Risk and Compliance, and ISO 27001 has been at the centre of most of it. I've taken organisations through the full implementation lifecycle, from scoping and risk assessment through the **Statement of Applicability**, internal audit, management review, and into Stage 1 and Stage 2 certification audits.

But I still remember my first ISO 27001 project. I had to figure out what the standard even was, clause by clause, template by template, mostly on my own. I spent weeks trying to work out the difference between a risk register and a risk treatment plan, why the Statement of Applicability mattered so much to the auditor, and what "documented information" actually meant in practice. Nobody handed me a map.

I want to make that path easier for the next person picking up their first ISO 27001 project. If that's you, new to information security and staring down an ISMS with no idea where to start, this is for you.

## What this series is

This is a straight-to-the-point implementation. No theory, no fluff. We're taking a fictional company, **MK Consults**, from zero to certification-ready, one phase at a time, with the real documents included: project charters, policies, registers, risk assessments, the Statement of Applicability, the lot.

Every post follows the same discipline an auditor would expect: what the activity is, which clause or control drives it, who owns it, what gets documented, and what evidence you retain. The goal isn't a binder of templates. It's an operational ISMS that holds up when a Stage 2 auditor starts pulling samples.

Follow along and lift the same approach into your own organisation. Swap in your names, your systems, your risks, and you've got a working implementation.

## How the series is structured

We'll work through the full lifecycle in order:

1. **Project Initiation:** the initial gap analysis, presenting the business case, securing management commitment, standing up the project, understanding context, and defining scope
2. **Planning:** detailed gap assessment, risk methodology, risk assessment, the Statement of Applicability, the risk treatment plan, and security objectives
3. **Implementation:** policies, procedures, control implementation, and awareness training
4. **Monitoring and Evaluation:** metrics, internal audit, management review, and corrective action
5. **Certification and Maintenance:** the Stage 1 documentation and readiness review, the Stage 2 evidence-based implementation audit, remediating any nonconformities they raise, then surveillance audits and recertification to keep the certificate
